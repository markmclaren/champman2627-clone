Quick Season on your phone

09.10.26
  • WebNew: Quick Season. Take over any club in the 22 playable leagues, pick your tactics and play a whole 2026/27 season in a minute, on your phone or anywhere else. It's a taster: the full game still needs a desktop or laptop.
  • WebQuick Season is now much closer to the real thing: pick your squad and tactics on CM 97/98-style screens (drag players to swap them, or use Auto Pick), check each player's page, then play every match with live commentary, a scorers log and substitutions. Injuries, tiredness and form mean you'll need to rotate the squad.
  • WebQuick Season now saves your season in your browser as you play: come back later and pick up where you left off with Continue. Fixtures also now alternate home and away properly through the season.
  • WebQuick Season gets tougher: choose Easy, Normal or Hard (the Classic 97/98 way, with no Auto Pick and no hints) when you start, make tactical changes and substitutions at half time, and keep the board happy or get the sack. Players now show their estimated value instead of a rating, and away teams wear their away kits when the colours clash.
  • WebQuick Season: players can now be booked and sent off, on both sides. A red card leaves a team a man down, and suspensions follow: 1 match for two bookings in a game, 3 for a straight red, and 1 for every fifth booking of the season. The half-time changes screen is simpler too: each player's position, name and condition on one line.
  • WebQuick Season: the board's confidence is no longer shown after each match. They still judge your results, so expect the sack if things go badly on Normal or Hard.
  • WebQuick Season has a main screen between matches, as in the original: Done for the next match, Squad Details, Competition Info (league table, your fixtures, every matchday's results), Board Confidence (the directors' verdict on your performance) and Settings.
  • WebQuick Season: Settings is now its own screen, as in the original, with match commentary speed, Start a New Game, and Credits. Have a look at the Credits.
  • WebQuick Season: screens now change with a quick fade to black, as in the original, instead of sliding.
  • WebQuick Season now has a photo behind every screen, pixelated like the original game's backgrounds, and Competition Info matches the original: Next Fixtures, Last Results and League Table.
  • WebQuick Season now uses the original game's own lettering (its MS Sans Serif and Lucida Sans bitmap fonts, pixel for pixel, at the original's sizes) and a pixelated photo behind every screen.

Known Issues

Weekdays can be one day out
The game runs on the 1997 calendar underneath, so the day of the week can be off by one.
Past winners still show 1985–1997
Competition history is built into the game file, so it still lists the 1990s winners, and a few clubs that no longer exist show as 'Minor Team'.
The game can't be played on phones or tablets
Use a computer with a keyboard and mouse. The rest of the site works on phones.
A League Cup second leg is occasionally played twice
Very occasionally a League Cup second leg is played a second time a couple of weeks later. It does no other harm to your game, and you can just play the extra match.
Lower-league squads can start short of players
Lower-league clubs can start with very few players, especially when you pick a smaller database or few nations (some League Two clubs had fewer than 5). Choose the largest database when starting a new game, and sign free agents to fill gaps.
Player values, wages and club finances out of balance across leagues
Player values and wage bills are too high in many leagues below the top flights, in England and abroad, so transfers are hard and some clubs run up big losses early in the season. A league-wide rebalance is planned; for now, free agents are the best way to strengthen, and releasing or selling fringe players helps with wages.
