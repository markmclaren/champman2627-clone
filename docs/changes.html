Gameplay Design & Scope Notes

Single-Season Tactical Simulation Scope
Quick Season is designed as a fast-playing, authentic match and season simulator. You manage your club's authentic 2026/27 squad through the complete league campaign. There is no multi-year transfer bidding engine, contracts, or youth regen scouting.
Squad Selection & Goalkeeper Requirement
Every matchday lineup must have 11 eligible starters with a goalkeeper occupying shirt 0. Players sidelined by injuries or red/yellow card bans cannot be picked until their suspension expires.
Browser LocalStorage Saves
Your active season save is automatically stored in your browser's local storage after each matchday. Clearing browser cache or site data will reset your save.
Difficulty Modes & Board Expectations
On Easy difficulty, the board will never sack you and Auto Pick is always available. On Normal and Hard (Classic 97/98), poor runs of form will prompt the directors to terminate your managerial contract.
