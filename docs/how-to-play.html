How to Play & FAQs

Everything you need to know about taking over a club, setting up your tactics, managing your squad, and surviving the season in Quick Season.

Getting Started

1. Choose a Country & League: Select from 22 playable divisions across 9 European nations (England, Scotland, Italy, Spain, Germany, France, Netherlands, Portugal, Belgium).

2. Pick Your Club: Clubs are ranked by squad strength (best eleven out of 200). Tap a club to inspect its rating, kit colors, and stadium.

3. Select Difficulty: Choose between Easy (patient board, Auto Pick enabled, best-fit player hints), Normal (standard expectations, realistic opponent boost), or Hard (Classic CM 97/98 with manual selection only and a strict board).

Tactics & Matchday Squad

Squad Setup: Each matchday you select 11 starters and up to 5 substitutes on the bench (16 shirts total, matching classic CM 97/98 rules).

Formations: Choose from authentic classic setups: 4-4-2, 4-3-3, 4-5-1, 3-5-2, 3-4-3, 5-3-2, or 5-4-1.

Tactical Styles:
• Balanced: Standard xG balance.
• Attacking: Increases attacking chances (+20% xG) but leaves you more vulnerable at the back (+15% opp xG).
• Defensive: Absorbs pressure against stronger sides (-15% xG for, -20% opp xG).

Fitness, Fatigue & Injuries

Physical Condition: Playing 90-minute matches drains condition based on stamina. Players under 80% condition suffer performance penalties; under 75% they become tired and risk injury.

Condition Recovery: Resting tired players between matchdays allows them to recover to 100%.

Injuries: Players can get injured in-match (scaled by their Injury proneness rating). Injured players cannot be picked until they recover.

Cards & Disciplinary Bans

Yellow & Red Cards: Aggressive players draw cards more frequently (scaled by their Aggression attribute).

Suspensions:
• Two bookings in a match = Red card + 1 match ban.
• Straight red card = 3 match ban.
• 5 accumulated yellow cards = 1 match ban.

10 Men: If a player is sent off, your team plays a man down for the rest of the game.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy or sell players? No. Quick Season is a fast-paced single-season tactical match simulator. You manage your chosen club's authentic 2026/27 squad through the entire season without a transfer market.
How do saves work? Your season is saved automatically in your web browser's local storage after every screen change. When you return to the game, tap Continue to resume right where you left off.
Can I get sacked? Yes, on Normal and Hard difficulties. The board evaluate your results against pre-season expectations. If your performance rating drops below acceptable levels, the board will terminate your contract.
How many substitutions can I make? You can make up to 3 substitutions per match from your 5 named bench players, either at half-time or when play halts for an injury.
Is an internet connection required? No. All code, fonts, and data assets are hosted locally on this site. Once loaded, the game runs entirely offline in client-side JavaScript.
