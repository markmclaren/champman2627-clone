100% Authentic 2026/27 Database

7,916 Real Players Across 394 Clubs: Every single youth placeholder entry has been completely eliminated. All 22 playable divisions now field authentic real-world squads with genuine player attributes, tactical suitability, and stamina/consistency ratings.

Full Lower Tier & Continental Coverage: Complete rosters populated from the Premier League and La Liga down to English League Two, Scottish League Two, Dutch Eerste Divisie, Portuguese Liga 2, Belgian Challenger Pro League, and continental B-teams.

Resolved Attribute Sentinels: Every player features balanced CM 97/98 playing skills (1–20), realistic £100m–£150m elite valuations (Haaland, Mbappé, Bellingham, Yamal), and zero sentinel artifacts.

Pure JavaScript Match & Season Engine

Live Match Commentary & Tactical Drama: Fast-paced Poisson-powered match simulation with minute-by-minute text commentary, momentum bars, substitutions, injuries, and yellow/red cards.

Authentic Tactical Styles: Classic CM 97/98 formations (4-4-2, 4-3-3, 3-5-2, 5-3-2, 4-5-1) with Balanced, Attacking (+20% xG / +15% opp xG), and Defensive styles.

League Top Scorers & Board Confidence: Player-specific scoring probabilities based on role and ability, season appearance/goal leaderboards, and realistic director confidence tracking.
